# Tawi Digital Solutions

**Building practical digital solutions for businesses.**

Tawi Digital Solutions is an early-stage, founder-led digital business being established by four co-founders. The team is bringing together different skills, ideas and interests to build useful digital services for businesses and individuals.

The business is starting lean: there is no guaranteed external funding, so the initial approach is to use the founders' existing skills and available resources, validate demand with real customers, and grow gradually.

> **Current stage:** Founding and validation. Services, pricing, roles, ownership documentation and operating processes are still being developed.

## Our direction

Tawi Digital Solutions aims to help businesses establish and improve their digital presence through practical, accessible services. We intend to begin with services that can be demonstrated, delivered and improved with a small team.

### Initial service ideas

These are proposed starting areas, not a claim that every service is already available.

1. **QR digital menus** — Mobile-friendly restaurant and hotel menus accessed through QR codes. The initial offer is a clear digital menu experience; online ordering and other advanced features may be explored after customer feedback.
2. **Website development** — Professional websites and landing pages for businesses and individuals.
3. **AI-assisted business solutions** — Helping businesses explore tools such as ChatGPT and Claude to improve workflows, customer communication and selected business operations.
4. **Digital business cards** — QR-enabled digital profiles that make it easier to share professional or business information.
5. **Promotional video and digital content** — Short-form promotional videos, adverts and social media content for businesses.

The team may propose additional services as it identifies customer needs and assesses its capacity to deliver them.

## Initial go-to-market approach

The first proposed customer-validation project is a QR digital menu for a local hotel or restaurant.

The intended sequence is:

1. Prepare and test a working demonstration.
2. Identify a suitable hotel or restaurant and present the idea.
3. Listen to the owner's needs and gather feedback.
4. Seek a first pilot customer and agree on a realistic scope.
5. Improve the service based on actual use and feedback.
6. Establish pricing and a repeatable delivery process.
7. Use the resulting work to create promotional content and approach more businesses.

The team also plans to create social media channels for Tawi Digital Solutions. Promotional videos and examples of its services can be published there to attract enquiries. AI tools may help review available performance data and suggest content improvements. Publishing, paid promotion and other external actions should remain subject to the team's review and approval.

## Founding team

Tawi Digital Solutions currently has **four co-founders**.

The founders are collecting their profiles and discussing how to allocate responsibilities across areas such as:

- Development and technical delivery
- Design and creative production
- Marketing and social media
- Sales, customer relationships and business development
- Operations, administration and finance

Final role assignments should be agreed by the founders and recorded in the project as decisions are made.

### Ownership and governance

The founders have discussed an ownership arrangement described as **“50-50”**, but the exact meaning and allocation among four individuals has not yet been clarified in the available founding discussion. No individual equity percentages are declared by this README.

Before treating ownership as final, the founders should document and agree on:
- Each founder's exact ownership percentage.
- Decision-making and voting arrangements.
- Roles, responsibilities and expected contributions.
- How expenses, revenue and profits will be handled.
- Intellectual property ownership and access to company accounts.
- What happens if a founder leaves or a new founder is considered.

Any future additions to the founding team will be considered separately and documented through an agreed process.

## Website and founders' portal

A company website is planned. One proposed feature is a private founders' area within the website.

The intended workflow is:
1. An administrator invites each co-founder using their email address.
2. Each founder signs in using an individual account.
3. Each founder completes their profile and provides information needed for the company website and internal records.
4. The administrator reviews the submitted information.
5. Approved founder profiles can then be used to populate the public team section of the website.

The founders' portal is a planned feature, not an existing or deployed system. It should use proper authentication and role-based access controls. Private founder information must not be exposed on public pages.

## How we plan to work

- Start with a focused, deliverable service rather than trying to launch every idea at once.
- Validate demand with real businesses before investing heavily in features.
- Work with the skills, devices, tools and resources currently available to the team.
- Keep costs low and make spending decisions transparently.
- Document decisions, responsibilities and important changes.
- Use AI as an assistant for research, content, analysis and productivity, with human review.
- Protect customer information, founder information, credentials and business accounts.
- Be honest about what is a demonstration, a pilot, in development or ready for customers.

## Collaboration and contribution

This repository is the shared starting point for the Tawi Digital Solutions founding team. Co-founders are invited to collaborate, discuss ideas, document decisions and contribute to the company's website and digital products.

### Suggested collaboration workflow

1. Read this README to understand the current direction.
2. Use GitHub Issues to propose ideas, record questions and track work.
3. Create a feature branch for changes rather than making unrelated changes directly to the default branch.
4. Open a pull request with a clear explanation of the change.
5. Review and discuss changes with the relevant co-founders before merging.

Repository access is managed by the repository owner. Each co-founder should share their GitHub username so an invitation can be sent. Invitations must be accepted through GitHub.
